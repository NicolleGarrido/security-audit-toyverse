# Risk Assessment

## Purpose

This risk assessment identifies the primary cybersecurity risks affecting Botium Toys based on the organization's current security posture and existing security controls.

The assessment follows the NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF) Identify function, focusing on asset management, risk identification, and security control evaluation.

---

# Overall Risk Score

**Risk Level:** High

**Score:** 8/10

The organization presents a high level of cybersecurity risk due to the absence of several critical administrative and technical security controls.

---

# Identified Risks

| Risk | Impact | Likelihood | Risk Level |
|------|---------|------------|------------|
| No encryption for payment data | High | High | Critical |
| No disaster recovery plan | High | High | Critical |
| No backups | High | High | Critical |
| Missing Intrusion Detection System (IDS) | High | Medium | High |
| Weak password management | Medium | High | High |
| Excessive user privileges | High | High | Critical |
| No separation of duties | Medium | High | High |
| Legacy systems require manual monitoring | Medium | Medium | Medium |

---

# Existing Security Controls

The following controls are currently implemented:

- Firewall
- Antivirus software
- Physical locks
- CCTV surveillance
- Fire detection and prevention systems
- Data integrity controls
- Data availability controls
- Privacy notification procedures for GDPR

These controls provide a basic level of protection but are insufficient to mitigate the organization's current risks.

---

# Missing Security Controls

The audit identified the absence of several important security controls:

- Least Privilege
- Separation of Duties
- Intrusion Detection System (IDS)
- Disaster Recovery Plan
- Data Backups
- Encryption
- Centralized Password Management

The absence of these controls significantly increases operational and compliance risks.

---

# Business Impact

If these risks are not addressed, Botium Toys may experience:

- Financial losses
- Service interruptions
- Data breaches
- Regulatory penalties
- Loss of customer trust
- Business continuity issues

---

# Risk Assessment Summary

The organization should prioritize implementing missing preventive, detective, and corrective controls to reduce cybersecurity risks and improve compliance with PCI DSS, GDPR, and SOC standards.

Immediate action is recommended due to the current high-risk posture.
