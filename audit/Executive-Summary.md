# Executive Summary

## Project Overview

This project presents an internal cybersecurity audit conducted for **ToyVerse Retail**, a fictional company specializing in online and physical toy sales.

The objective of this audit is to evaluate the organization's current cybersecurity posture by reviewing administrative, technical, and physical security controls, identifying potential risks, and assessing compliance with industry standards.

## Objectives

- Assess existing security controls.
- Identify vulnerabilities and operational risks.
- Evaluate compliance with cybersecurity frameworks and regulations.
- Recommend security improvements.
- Strengthen the organization's overall security posture.

## Frameworks Used

- NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF)
- PCI DSS
- GDPR
- SOC 2

## Audit Scope

The audit covers:

- Internal network
- Employee devices
- Servers
- Customer information
- Payment processing
- Physical security
- Data storage
- Legacy systems

## Overall Risk Rating

**High Risk**

The organization lacks several critical security controls, including encryption, disaster recovery planning, intrusion detection, and centralized password management.

Immediate corrective actions are recommended.
