# Executive Summary

## Project Overview

This project presents the results of an internal cybersecurity audit conducted for Botium Toys, a fictional retail company expanding its online operations.

The assessment was performed using the NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF) to identify security gaps, evaluate existing controls, assess regulatory compliance, and recommend improvements to strengthen the organization's overall security posture.

---

## Audit Objectives

The primary objectives of this audit were:

- Assess existing security controls.
- Identify cybersecurity risks and vulnerabilities.
- Evaluate compliance with industry standards and regulations.
- Recommend security improvements based on identified risks.

---

## Audit Scope

The audit included the evaluation of:

- Internal network infrastructure
- Employee devices
- Servers and information systems
- Customer information
- Payment processing environment
- Data storage
- Physical security controls
- Legacy systems

---

## Methodology

The assessment was based on:

- NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF)
- Risk Assessment
- Controls Assessment
- Compliance Review

The following compliance standards were considered:

- PCI DSS
- GDPR
- SOC Type 1
- SOC Type 2

---

## Overall Risk Rating

**High Risk (8/10)**

The organization currently lacks several essential cybersecurity controls required to adequately protect sensitive information and maintain regulatory compliance.

Critical findings include:

- Lack of encryption for sensitive payment information
- No disaster recovery plan
- Absence of data backups
- No Intrusion Detection System (IDS)
- Weak password management practices
- Excessive user access permissions
- Missing implementation of Least Privilege and Separation of Duties

Although firewall protection, antivirus software, physical security, and data availability controls are implemented, several critical security gaps remain.

---

## Conclusion

Based on the assessment, Botium Toys should prioritize implementing missing administrative, technical, and operational controls to reduce organizational risk, improve regulatory compliance, and strengthen its cybersecurity posture.
